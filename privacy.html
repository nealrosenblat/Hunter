Hammer & Delgado: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version

Hammer & Delgado does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (which scene you reached and your case-file scores) on your own device so you can continue later. This save never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Network access

The app contains everything it needs and plays fully offline. It does not send data to any server.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game's support page.
