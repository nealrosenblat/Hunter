Tonight on HAMMER & DELGADO...

A late-90s-style 3D game about a 1980s TV cop show that never existed. Rex Hammer has a mullet, a .44 Magnum named Gerald, and a brown Brougham held together with duct tape. His partner Dee Dee Delgado is much more competent. Their captain would like ONE week without a fruit cart incident.

PLAY IT YOUR WAY

• Touch controls designed for iPhone and iPad.

LIKE YOU REMEMBER IT
